The pipeline

Three distinct stages — only the middle one is where learning happens.
STAGE 1 · DETERMINISTIC
Physics baseline
Q=m·Cp·ΔT and Q_reb=(R+1)Dλ give the theoretical minimum. No learning here.
STAGE 2 · HIERARCHICAL REGRESSION
Expected-baseline model
Adjusts for ambient temperature, equipment fouling and product — pooled across the cluster, re-weighted toward plant-specific data over time.
STAGE 3 · CONTROL LIMITS
Residual & anomaly flag
Actual minus expected, checked against a statistical control limit for that reactor+product.
STAGE 4 · GROUPING
Pattern surfaced
Flagged batches grouped by reactor/shift/product reveal the recurring cause.
